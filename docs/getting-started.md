Welcome to **Redacted Skies** — your friendly neighborhood clearinghouse for the idea that something (or someone) has been sharing our atmosphere for a long time, and that the paperwork is finally catching up.

This site is entertainment with a dossier aesthetic. We remix public lore, speculative fiction, and the cultural mythology of “unidentified aerial phenomena” into readable case files. No real people are targeted. No actual secrets are spilled. The black bars are theatrical.

## How to read the files

Every dossier on Redacted Skies follows a loose schema:

1. **Header stamp** — classification theater (`PUBLIC`, `REDACTED`, `SPECULATIVE`).
2. **Meta block** — monospace timestamps, coordinates, and case IDs.
3. **Narrative** — what allegedly happened, told with a wink.
4. **Redactions** — black bars (`████`) stand in for “still classified” flavor text. They are props.
5. **Analyst note** — our in-universe commentary. Always labeled as speculation.

### Reading tips

- Treat every page like a museum placard for a conspiracy exhibit — fun first, claims second.
- Cross-links between sightings, crash files, and the timeline are intentional rabbit holes.
- If a paragraph feels too certain, look for the nearest redaction bar or analyst disclaimer.
