# About Redacted Skies

<p class="classification">Classification: PUBLIC // CLEARANCE: ANYONE</p>

<div class="file-stamp">MANIFESTO</div>

**Redacted Skies** is a static site for people who like their conspiracy aesthetic with a smile. We explore the idea that aliens are real and existent — as culture, as story, as a delicious “what if” — without punching down on real people or pretending we hold a master key to the hangar.

## The brand

- **Name:** Redacted Skies  
- **Tone:** Intriguing, slightly tongue-in-cheek, declassified-file chic  
- **Thesis:** The skies aren’t empty  
- **Promise:** Entertainment first; speculation labeled; no real secrets claimed

## What this is

A VitePress-powered archive of sample dossiers, crash-site daydreams, and a disclosure timeline you can read on the couch. Built to deploy as static HTML — no API, no login, no tracking of your clearance level.

## What this isn’t

- Not investigative journalism  
- Not a dump of real classified material  
- Not a place that targets private individuals  
- Not mean-spirited
