## What the experts concluded

In **June**, **Sara Osborn** captured video and stills of a floating, jellyfish-like object with apparent tentacles near **Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson** in Alaska. It disappeared within a few minutes.

On *Jesse Weber Live*, **UFO Witness** host **Ben Hansen** said he believes the images are genuine and were captured in camera, but he and former **UAP Task Force** analyst **Sarah Gamm** lean toward a mundane explanation: a kite or balloon-type object. Hansen noted military exercises with high-altitude balloon launches in the area around that time, and conceded it doesn’t look like an off-the-shelf item.

Gamm: “Officially, I would call it unidentified, but most likely (it’s) a high-altitude balloon.”
