The **Sources** desk collects **real external articles and outbound links** — press clippings, science writeups, and reputable reporting that touch the skies, exoplanets, magnetism, and the hunt for habitable worlds.

These pages are **not** invented case files. They summarize accurately, link out to the original, and may use light dossier framing (“signal intercepted,” classification stamps) without claiming alien life or inventing facts.

Fictional archives live under [Sightings](/sightings/) and [Crash Sites](/crash-sites/). Keep them separate.

1. One markdown page per article (slug from topic).
2. Frontmatter: title, date, outlet, author, externalUrl, tags.
3. Accurate summary of what researchers/journalists concluded — no alien overclaims.
4. Prominent outbound link to the original; never paste the full article.
