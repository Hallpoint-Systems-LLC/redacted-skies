## What the researchers concluded

Using South Africa’s **MeerKAT** radio telescope array, an international team detected **variable radio bursts** originating from **Beta Pictoris b**, a massive gas giant (~10–12× Jupiter’s mass) orbiting Beta Pictoris about **63 light-years** from Earth.

Key points from the reporting:

- **First confirmed radio signal from a single exoplanet** — isolated to the planet itself, not the whole star system.
- The host star is **magnetically quiet**, which helped separate the planet’s faint emission from stellar noise.
- The physics matches **auroral / magnetic-field mechanisms** familiar from Jupiter and other Solar System gas giants (charged particles along field lines → aurora-linked radio emission).
- The detection implies a magnetic field **thousands of times stronger than Earth’s**.
- Landmark for **exoplanet science and habitability research**: magnetic fields help protect atmospheres from stellar winds. The article is explicit that this is **not** a detection of E.T. / alien life.
